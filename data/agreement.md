**Rights holder's consent:** Artist A, the creator and rights holder,
agrees to the sale of **up to 10 digital editions** of the work. This
consent is recorded in the registry and the deal can't exceed it.

## What we agree

1. **Editions.** There will be **10** digital editions, numbered 1 to 10.
2. **Price.** Each edition is first sold for **KES 15,000.00**.
3. **Currency.** All amounts are in Kenyan shillings (KES). Amounts are
   counted in whole cents; anything smaller is rounded down.
4. **Who shares, and how much.** Every first sale is split between us:

   | Co-owner | Role | Share |
   |---|---|---|
   | Artist A | Artist | 60% |
   | BASIX.MARKET | Platform | 15% |
   | GTM Broker C | Go-to-market broker | 15% |
   | Print Studio D | Physical production | 10% |

   That's 100% in total.
5. **Resales.** Whenever an edition is resold, **10%** of the resale
   price is a royalty to us, split in the same proportions as above. The
   seller keeps the other 90%.
6. **No double charge.** BASIX.MARKET takes no separate resale fee. It
   earns only through its share of the royalty.
7. **Rounding.** Each person's share is rounded down to the cent. Any
   cents left over go to the artist, so every sale pays out exactly what
   was paid.
8. **Payments.** Each of us is paid to the wallet in the registry under our
   name.
9. **Changes.** These terms can't be changed once they're locked. A change
   means a new version, and every one of us has to approve it again. Sales
   already paid stay as they were paid.
10. **Watching.** If an edition is resold somewhere that doesn't pay the
    royalty, we'll be told what's owed and why. A transfer with no payment
    (a gift, say) isn't treated as an unpaid sale.

## Signatures

Each co-owner approves this exact version in the app. The approval records
