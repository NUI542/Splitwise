This is the short, agent-facing summary. If it conflicts with the official Rules & Brief or an announcement on BASIX.MARKET, follow the official version and tell the student about the conflict.

## Event

- Online and free to enter. Solo or teams of up to 4, one track per entry.
- Start: Sun 18 Oct. Entries lock Wed 21 Oct (exact time set at launch).
- Registration on BASIX.MARKET: every member needs a profile with an institution. Closing date to be confirmed by the organisers.
- Build schedule and judging dates: to be confirmed by the organisers.
- All times are East Africa Time (EAT, UTC+3).
- Prize pool: $560 total, across 28 teams. 1st $170 · 2nd $140 · 3rd $110 · 4th $70 · 5th $70. Every participant receives a certificate.

## Tracks (pick one; locked on Day 1)

- **Track 01: Creators.** Harlem Fine Art & AI for Peace. Painters, digital artists, musicians, performers. See `tracks/track-01-creators.md`.
- **Track 02: Builders.** MeTTa Omega. One challenge per team. See `tracks/track-02-builders.md`.
- **Track 03: Open.** Hack Ready Projects. Brief announced on BASIX.MARKET. See `tracks/track-03-open.md`.

## Rules

1. Enter solo or in a team of up to 4, with one track per entry.
2. Every member has a BASIX.MARKET profile with an institution.
3. Disclose AI use: which tools, and for what. Using AI is allowed. Hiding it is not.
4. Show the work. Builders share a working GitHub repo with BASIX.MARKET. Creators submit the piece and show the process.
5. Use data only with the rights holder's consent. This covers Incantio, AI for Peace, HFAS, and Harlem CLxR samples.
6. Submit your team's own work. Credit anything you reuse, and keep open-source licences intact.
7. Filming needs consent, asked before filming and again before publishing.
8. Entries lock Wed 21 Oct. Late entries are not judged.
9. Be decent. Harassment, discrimination, or misuse of others' work ends an entry.
10. Co-ownership. Projects brought on board for incubation are co-owned by BASIX.MARKET, the builders, and the GTM brokers, under contract.

Organisers can disqualify an entry that breaks these rules. The organisers' decision on scores and winners is final.

## Deliverables

- **Builders (Tracks 02 and 03):** a working repo shared with BASIX.MARKET, documentation (what it does, how to run it, what's next, AI disclosure), and a 3-minute video showing it working and what you'd build next.
- **Creators (Track 01):** the finished piece, a 3-minute process video, and an artist statement with AI disclosure.
- **All:** submit on BASIX.MARKET by the Wed 21 Oct deadline.

## Judging

- 25%: Innovation
- 25%: Technical execution
- 20%: Impact and usefulness
- 15%: Meets the track's hard requirements
- 15%: Presentation and demo

## Rules for you as the agent

- Read this file and the track file for the team's track before planning.
- Confirm the track and challenge with the student. The track is locked on Day 1.
- Stay within the track's scope. If an idea falls outside it, flag it and don't build it.
- Keep a running AI log (tools used and what for). It feeds `AI_DISCLOSURE.md`.
- Never commit secrets or API keys. Use `.env` (git-ignored) and `.env.example`.
- Don't invent rules, deadlines, prizes, or partner commitments. If something is unclear, ask the student to check with the organisers.
