# Track 02: MeTTa Omega (Builders)

Choose one challenge per team. Challenges run from MeTTa Foundation (Level 1) to Advanced.

**Eligibility note:** the deck lists "teams with prior hackathon or Sprint experience, or a completed MeTTa track or BASIX.MARKET coursework" as who the track is for. Check with the organisers whether this is a requirement or a suggestion before the team starts.

## MeTTa Foundation (Level 1)

1. **The Glass Box Agent.** Every decision comes with a reasoning trail that anyone can challenge.
   - Partner brief (HFAS): a jury assistant that shows why an artwork was selected.
2. **The Agent That Grows Up.** Start with a small rule set and show it learning visibly. Show the diff.
   - Partner brief (Incantio): a composing agent that learns a style and writes the split sheet.
3. **MeTTa Core Challenge.** Build one small feature: patterns, knowledge, inference, or rule learning.
   - Partner brief (AI for Peace): a knowledge graph of the AI for Peace artworks and artists.

## Omega AI Agents (Levels 1–3)

4. **Two Agents, One Truth.** Two agents disagree about a fact and reason to a shared answer.
   - Partner brief (AI for Peace): two communities' accounts of one event, mediated into one story.
5. **The Agent Without Borders.** Reasoning that works across languages, literacy levels, and connectivity.
   - Partner brief (Harlem CLxR): carry artist statements between Harlem and Nairobi.
6. **BASIX.MARKET: Mature Build.** A working module for the BASIX NFT Marketplace.
   - Partner brief (AI for Peace): phigital editions of AI for Peace works, with royalties to the artists.

## Advanced: Next Level Devs

7. **Trusted With Money.** A business decision with an audit trail that a manager could override.
   - Partner brief (HFAS): approve fractional-ownership and royalty payouts for #BuyBlackArt.
8. **The NPC That Won't Break Character.** A character with real memory that never contradicts itself.
   - Partner brief (Incantio): a band-mate that remembers every co-write and what each person is owed.

## Advanced: AI Infrastructure Layer

9. **Build the AI Infrastructure Layer.** Live MVP work on GitHub with Omega. Scope options include scale support, a CTO agent, or real-world-asset rails.
   - Partner brief (Harlem CLxR): cultural-IP and real-world-asset rails for Harlem ↔ Nairobi.

## Deliverables

- A working repo shared with BASIX.MARKET.
- Documentation, including AI disclosure.
- A 3-minute video.

## Stack

MeTTa, Hyperon, and Omega. Resources are on BASIX.MARKET.

## Pairing

A Track 02 team can pair with a Track 01 artist or musician found on the BASIX job board. Both teams can win.

## Judging

- 30%: Technical / creative execution
- 25%: Clarity of the 3-minute video or demo
- 25%: Fit to track
- 20%: Team collaboration and documentation
- Also judged: "would a real business, or a real artist, trust this?"

## Guidance for the agent

- Partner briefs are proposals until the partner confirms them. Say so if the team builds around one.
- Keep the reasoning visible in the output. For challenges 1, 4, and 7, a judge should be able to see why the system decided what it did.
