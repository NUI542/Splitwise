# Track 01: Harlem Fine Art & AI for Peace (Creators)

**For:** painters, illustrators, digital artists, musicians, producers, and performers making work with AI for peace.
**Theme:** Art as Healing. History as Power. Legacy in Motion.

## Challenges (choose one)

1. **AI as creative partner.** Make a work with AI as co-creator, and show the conversation.
2. **Legacy in motion.** A work, song, or performance that evolves with its audience.
3. **The borderless studio.** Two artists, Harlem ↔ Nairobi, one piece made in real time.
4. **Peace, love, unity.** A piece in the spirit of the AI for Peace collection.
5. **Co-own your work.** A phigital piece with on-chain provenance that keeps paying the artist, the platform, and the broker.

## Deliverables

- The finished piece.
- A 3-minute process video.
- An artist statement with AI disclosure.

## Musicians

- Musicians can list finished tracks on incantio.com, which is artist-owned and artist-priced. Each track gets its own licence link, and every co-writer's split is shown.
- Challenge wording is a proposal. Confirm it with Incantio before building around it.

## Scope notes

- The work must be yours or your team's.
- Works from the AI for Peace collection may only be referenced with permission, and their artists must be credited.

## Guidance for the agent

- Help the artist keep a process record of prompts, iterations, and tool use. The process video and AI disclosure both depend on it.
- Keep the artist's own voice in the artist statement. Draft options if asked, but don't write it for them.
